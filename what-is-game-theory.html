PRIVATE OFFLINE REVIEW

One table. Seven social-strategy games.

GameTheory explores choices whose outcomes depend on what others choose. In this version, you play with three computer-controlled characters, not other people. A session selects three games from seven possibilities.

  • Shoal: share a fish stock without exhausting it.
  • Collection: bid for a collection using a limited budget.
  • City: negotiate a split while keeping a shared city standing.
  • Signal: choose a cooperative hunt or an individual payoff.
  • Envelope: decide how much to send and return in a trust exchange.
  • Constellation: coordinate safe and risky network contributions.
  • Lantern: keep a light burning while a hidden saboteur works against the group.

What this private version changes

Original bitmap art is replaced with geometric review placeholders. The recorded music and score are omitted. There is no login, AI API, cloud account, live messaging or backend session upload.

Your nickname, optional profile details, chats and session history may be saved in this browser. Use a nickname and avoid personal or sensitive details. The game includes local history controls; browser storage behavior varies, especially when opening files directly. Copy buttons can place selected report text on your clipboard.
